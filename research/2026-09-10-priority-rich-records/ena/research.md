# ENA rich-record research draft

Prepared 2026-09-10. **Research only: nothing applied.** `draft.json` is a complete six-language candidate for `ena`; its proposed rich depth is conditional on the current contract release and creation of the eight minimal endpoint dependencies. Canonical ENA remains thin. `endpoint-drafts.json` contains independently applicable content bodies for root deduplication, not a bulk launch API request.

The candidate contains 10 descriptors (2 types, 6 formats, 2 standards), 7 access paths (4 Read, 3 Write), 1 metric, 18 incident edges (7 retained/researched, 11 proposed additions), no gallery items and no operational routes. Passing structural checks is not human acceptance.

## Identity, preservation and evidence

Fresh authenticated Record API searches and `GET /api/records/ena?include=localizations,sources,edges,routes` confirmed `ena` as a system and its seven existing relationships. Initial record timestamp: `2026-09-10T20:02:28.495Z`. Snapshots are in `canonical-record.json`, `canonical-prevalidation.json`, `canonical-search.json` and `endpoint-search.json`. There were no existing routes or gallery assets to remove. No unrelated records were edited.

Principal sources are the operator-authored [2025 annual update](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/D1/D120/8363843) (published 2025-12-03, in the January 2026 issue), [2024 annual update](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/53/D1/D49/7903379), official [INSDC participation/governance statement](https://www.insdc.org/about-insdc/), [ENA retrieval documentation](https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/retrieval/programmatic-access.html), and [submission guidance](https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submit/general-guide.html). All newly authored sources have six translated titles and access date 2026-09-10.

## Corrections to the backlog leads

- **Ocean origin is representable.** The INSDC location vocabulary accepts countries, oceans and seas. ENA migrated the sequence qualifier from `/country` to `/geo_loc_name` in 2024. The current [INSDC feature table, v11.4, April 2026](https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/) defines the sequence fields. Sample field names differ. Thus the backlog's assertion that a country field necessarily excludes high-seas origin is false. Determining jurisdiction from these fields still requires a separate assessment; reviewed technical documents did not establish a BBNJ batch-notification or benefit-sharing status field. [2024 update](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/53/D1/D49/7903379), [minimum metadata policy](https://www.insdc.org/news/insdc-spatiotemporal-metadata-minimum-standards-update-03-03-2023/).
- **Mandatory does not mean complete.** The policy phased in new BioSample requirements by May 2023 and sequence requirements by December 2024, with explicit exceptions and missing-value terms. Do not treat all historic records as complete provenance or assume that geographic metadata proves a legal status. [INSDC policy](https://www.insdc.org/news/insdc-spatiotemporal-metadata-minimum-standards-update-03-03-2023/).
- **BioSamples now owns sample management.** The 2025 update reports transfer of validation, storage, accessioning and partner metadata exchange to BioSamples, with ENA workflows retaining seamless submission. This is implemented metadata integration, not just a cross-reference. [2025 update](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/D1/D120/8363843).
- **ELIXIR recognition is not a system-to-organization membership edge.** Core Data Resource status is retained in profile prose; no invented `part_of` or invalid membership endpoints were added. [Blue-Cloud's ENA description](https://blue-cloud.org/data-infrastructures/european-nucleotide-archive-ena).

## Access and standards

Working public entry points are Browser, Portal API (metadata search in JSON/TSV), Browser API (XML/FASTA/EMBL record retrieval), and the public run/analysis file server. The examples in `live-checks.json` include a real XML Tara project, JSON query response and live MIxS checklist. Public retrieval does not require a personal account/API key. Free Webin submission requires an account; interactive, REST V2 and CLI paths are distinct. Assemblies require Webin-CLI according to current guidance. Uploading files, successful validation and public release are separate events. Private Data Hub access is excluded from the unrestricted public path. [Retrieval](https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/retrieval/programmatic-access.html), [Webin REST V2](https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submit/general-guide/programmatic.html), [Webin-CLI](https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submit/general-guide/webin-cli.html).

INSDC is scoped to sequence-feature and qualifier specifications; MIxS is scoped to sample checklists, with [ERC000014](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/api/xml/ERC000014) directly retrieved. Neither is claimed to certify every archived record. FAIR, ELIXIR designation, licence terms, BioSample identifiers and NCBI Taxonomy names were not invented as new standard tags. BAM/CRAM are documented but not approved format IDs; they remain outside the format tags. Formats are encodings, not API/FTP labels. No vocabulary changes are proposed.

Aspera and Globus were investigated in the [file-download guide](https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/retrieval/file-download.html). They have additional client/authentication considerations and were not represented as universally credential-free access. Broker participation is explained in the profile/account guidance; the dedicated broker-document URL returned 403 and was not used as a separate access row. No operational route was previously approved, so `routes` remains empty; the concrete APIs are documented as access paths for root's later routing decision.

## All six relationship review outcomes

| Type | Outcome, evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| `governs` | Reviewed EMBL Council, EMBL, EMBL-EBI, ENA SAB and INSDC committees. Canonical graph already has `embl-council → embl → embl-ebi` governance and executive/implementation committee governance of `insdc-members`. [EMBL governance](https://www.embl.org/governance/) establishes institutional authority; [INSDC](https://www.insdc.org/about-insdc/) establishes shared policy authority. No additional direct ENA governance edge is proposed: the evidence belongs to those institutional/collective layers. [ENA scientific advisers](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/about/leadership/advisory-boards/) are not treated as binding governance, and individual states/funders are not given unilateral authority. |
| `operates` | Retain `embl-ebi → ena`, grounded in the operator-authored annual update. Other partners exchange data or finance activities; no evidence establishes them as ENA co-operators. |
| `funds` | Propose incoming edges from EMBL, EU, BBSRC, Wellcome, Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation, NIAID and Novo Nordisk Foundation, each scoped to work and awards named in the [2025 funding statement](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/D1/D120/8363843). These acknowledge reported project support, not an archive-wide budget or a claim that all awards remain active in September 2026. Legal grantee, ENA allocation, amounts and exact award periods are unverified; no intermediary is invented. |
| `member_of` | Retain system participation `ena → insdc`, distinguishing participating archives from the existing organizational members. ELIXIR Core Data Resource and Global Core Biodata Resource recognition are designations, not evidence for additional membership edges. |
| `publishes_to` | Propose `wellcome-sanger-institute → ena` for documented Tree of Life genome deposition in its [2022/23 report](https://www.sanger.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/WSI_Annual_Highlights_2023_Digital.pdf), corroborated by [Aquatic Symbiosis release policy](https://www.sanger.ac.uk/collaboration/aquatic-symbiosis-genomics-project/). Also propose `genoscope → ena`: live [PRJEB402 XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/api/xml/PRJEB402) names Genoscope CEA, first public 2012-08-22, last updated 2026-09-08. This covers that Tara umbrella project metadata, not exclusive authorship of every linked dataset. |
| `syncs_to` | Retain four directional ENA↔GenBank and ENA↔DDBJ edges. [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/) explicitly states daily exchange; [INSDC](https://www.insdc.org/about-insdc/) includes corrections. No last-transfer timestamp or immediate/complete private-record replication is asserted. Retain ENA→MGnify for selective analysis inputs; [MGnify guidance dated 2026-07-13](https://docs.mgnify.org/src/docs/dataflow.html) describes validation, eligibility, requests and explicit permission for private inputs. Add ENA→BioSamples for submitted metadata registration and BioSamples→ENA for authoritative metadata use, scoped to the implemented workflow reported in 2025. |

Additional examined candidates were withheld: OBIS guidance [links derived occurrence records back to INSDC deposits](https://obis.org/2024/10/22/obis-edna/) but does not establish a direct reciprocal ENA/OBIS data-transfer workflow. No primary evidence established the backlog's WoRMS→ENA transfer; a taxon hyperlink alone would not suffice. Blue-Cloud describes discovery/retrieval integration but the consulted page does not identify a concrete replicated dataset, cadence or separate approved destination route. BBNJ/CHM is prospective context, not a verified operating transfer. ENA's many submitters are not reduced to a fictitious single publisher, and no generic publish edge was created for all users.

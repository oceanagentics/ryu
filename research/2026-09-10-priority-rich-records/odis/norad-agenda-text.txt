Subject: Twenty-eighth Session of the IOC Committee on International 
Oceanographic Data and Information Exchange (IODE-28) and Third 
International Ocean Data Conference (IODC-3), Santa Marta, Colombia, 
10–14 March 2025 
On behalf of the IODE Co-chairs, Dr Paula Correa Sierra and Ms Lotta Fyrberg, I have the pleasure 
of inviting you to participate in the 2 8th intergovernmental s ession of the IOC Committee on 
International Oceanographic Data and Information Exchange (IODE), to be held from 12 to 14 March 
2025 at the Instituto de Investigaciones Marinas y Costeras (INVEMAR), Santa Marta, Colombia. 
For further information and access to the documentation, please visit the meeting's website  at 
https://oceanexpert.org/event/4258. The provisional agenda and the timetable of the session and 
conference are attached. 
The IODE session will be preceded on 10–11 March 202 5 by the 3 rd International Ocean Data 
Conference (IODC-3), which will focus on new developments in the field of oceanographic data and 
information management and exchange. 
We wish to draw your attention to the provisional list of participants  on 
https://oceanexpert.org/event/4258#participants, which is based on the official lists of IODE NODC 
contact points, IODE ADU contact points, IODE AIU contact points, IODE national coordinators for 
data management, IODE national coordinators for marine information management.

We would appreciate being informed by the IOC focal points  of the composition of the national 
delegations attending the IODE session and/or IODC-3 conference. If possible, please provide full 
names and OceanExpert identifiers (e.g. https://oceanexpert.org/expert/6552). The Secretariat will 
include all names provided in the list of participants for the session. 
IODE NODC contact points, IODE ADU contact points, IODE AIU contact points, IODE national 
coordinators for data management, IODE national coordinators for marine information management 
who plan to attend the events should ensure that their names are included in the official 
communication sent by the IOC focal point. 
The scientific conference preceding the session will allow members of the IODE community (and 
invited guests) to present new challenges and opportunities in the area of oceanographic data and 
information management and exchange. A call for abstracts will be sent out shortly. Information on 
the conference and its programme will be made available through  
https://oceanexpert.org/event/4257 and https://oceandataconference.org.  
To enable us to make the necessary arrangements for the session , your re sponse before 11 
December 2024 will be appreciated. Responses should be addressed to Mr Peter Pissierssens, 
Head of the IOC Project Office for IODE (e-mail: p.pissierssens@unesco.org). I would like to remind 
you that the participation of national delegations in IODE sessions is self-funded and is part of the 
national contribution to the IODE programme.  Funding for participation in the IODC -3 may be 
available.  
With the assurances of my highest consideration, I remain, 
  Yours sincerely,

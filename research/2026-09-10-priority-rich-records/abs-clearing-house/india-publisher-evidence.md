# India publication role evidence

Primary government-hosted indexed PDF text, *Implementation of India’s National Biodiversity Action Plan: An Overview* (2019), chapter 14, printed page 153, distinguishes:

- “National Publishing Authority- MoEFCC”
- “National Authorized User for ABS Clearing-House-NBA”

The same role list names MoEFCC as national focal point and NBA as competent national authority. This supports a historical 2019 designation, not a claim about the present organizational assignment.
